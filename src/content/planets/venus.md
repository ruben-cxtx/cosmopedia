Venus is the second planet from the Sun and Earth's nearest neighbor in size and composition, yet the two worlds could not be more different. Its thick atmosphere of carbon dioxide creates a runaway greenhouse effect, making it the hottest planet in the solar system with surface temperatures averaging 465°C — hot enough to melt lead. Dense clouds of sulfuric acid shroud the planet, making it the brightest natural object in Earth's night sky aside from the Moon. Venus rotates extremely slowly and in the opposite direction to most planets, meaning a Venusian day is longer than its year.
