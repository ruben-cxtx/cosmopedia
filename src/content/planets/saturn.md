Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second largest in the solar system. It is best known for its spectacular ring system — the most extensive of any planet — composed of billions of ice and rock particles ranging in size from tiny grains to house-sized boulders. Despite its enormous size, Saturn is the least dense planet, composed mostly of hydrogen and helium with a density lower than water. Its moon Titan is the only moon in the solar system with a substantial atmosphere and standing bodies of liquid, making it a fascinating analog for early Earth. Enceladus, another moon, ejects water vapor and ice from subsurface geysers, hinting at a liquid ocean beneath its icy crust.
