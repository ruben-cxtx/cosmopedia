Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and the third largest by diameter. It is classified as an ice giant, with a composition dominated by water, methane, and ammonia ices beneath a hydrogen-helium atmosphere. Its striking cyan-blue color comes from methane in the upper atmosphere, which absorbs red light. Uranus is unique in that its rotational axis is tilted nearly 98 degrees, effectively orbiting the Sun on its side — likely the result of a massive collision billions of years ago. This extreme tilt creates the most unusual seasons in the solar system, with each pole experiencing decades of continuous sunlight or darkness.
