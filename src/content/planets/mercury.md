Mercury is the smallest planet in the solar system and the closest to the Sun, orbiting at just 0.39 AU. Despite its proximity to the Sun, it is not the hottest planet — that title belongs to Venus, whose thick atmosphere traps heat. Mercury's surface is heavily cratered and resembles Earth's Moon, shaped by billions of years of impacts with no geological activity to erase them. Its iron core is enormous relative to its size, making up about 85% of the planet's radius. The extreme temperature swings between night and day are among the most dramatic in the solar system.
