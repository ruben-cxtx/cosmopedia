Neptune is the eighth and farthest known planet from the Sun in the solar system. It is an ice giant similar in composition to Uranus, but denser and bluer — its deep azure color is enhanced by trace amounts of methane and unknown atmospheric compounds. Neptune generates tremendous internal heat, radiating about 2.6 times more energy than it receives from the Sun, which drives the most violent weather in the solar system. Its largest moon, Triton, is one of the coldest objects in the solar system and is almost certainly a captured Kuiper Belt object, destined to eventually break apart or crash into Neptune. Neptune was predicted mathematically in 1846 by Urbain Le Verrier before being directly observed.
