Ceres is the largest object in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter, and the closest dwarf planet to the Sun. Discovered in 1801 by Giuseppe Piazzi, it has been classified at various times as a planet, an asteroid, and finally a dwarf planet. Ceres is a rounded, differentiated body with a rocky core and an icy mantle, and possibly a subsurface ocean. The Dawn spacecraft revealed a geologically active world with bright salt deposits in impact craters, formed when subsurface briny water was excavated and evaporated. The enigmatic Ahuna Mons, a lone mountain rising 4 km from an otherwise flat plain, appears to be a cryovolcano — a volcano that erupts ice and salt instead of lava.
