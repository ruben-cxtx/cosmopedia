Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and the second smallest in the solar system. Known as the Red Planet, its iconic color comes from iron oxide (rust) covering its surface. Despite being only half the size of Earth, Mars has roughly the same land surface area since it has no oceans. It is the most explored planet beyond Earth, hosting multiple active rovers and orbiters. Billions of years ago, Mars had liquid water, a thicker atmosphere, and conditions that may have supported microbial life.
