Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the largest of the terrestrial planets. It is the only world known to support life, thanks to a rare combination of liquid water, a nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere, plate tectonics, and a magnetic field that protects the surface from solar wind. The planet's surface is constantly reshaped by geological forces — volcanoes, earthquakes, erosion, and the slow drift of tectonic plates. Earth's single large Moon stabilizes its axial tilt, which helps maintain relatively stable climatic conditions over geological timescales. From space, Earth appears as the "Blue Marble," one of the most iconic images in history.
