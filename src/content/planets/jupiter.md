Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and by far the largest in the solar system — more than twice as massive as all other planets combined. It is a gas giant with no solid surface, composed primarily of hydrogen and helium. Its iconic banded appearance comes from powerful jet streams in the atmosphere that separate into light zones and dark belts. The Great Red Spot, a storm wider than Earth, has been raging for centuries. Jupiter's four Galilean moons — Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto — are worlds in their own right, with Europa's subsurface ocean making it one of the most exciting targets in the search for extraterrestrial life.
